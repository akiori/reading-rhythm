# Focused Reading for Long Research Notes

Long technical writing becomes harder to follow when every sentence carries the same visual weight. A deliberate **visual rhythm** gives the reader small, stable places to re-enter the argument.

The goal is not to turn prose into a word cloud. Instead, the page keeps the original order, preserves **working memory**, and makes a useful **re-entry point** visible after attention drifts.

- Keep paragraphs intact.
- Use a few strong anchors, not constant bold text.
- Let typography guide attention without competing with the content.

> The reader should be able to return to the line of thought without starting the page again.

| Principle | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Visual rhythm | Faster re-entry |
| Stable wording | Less cognitive overhead |
| Limited emphasis | Clearer hierarchy |
